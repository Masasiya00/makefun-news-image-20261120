# The World in One Day — Friday 20 November 2026

One image that sums up the world's news of Friday 20 November 2026: 45
stories from 7 regions, built for make.fun's
[news-image-20261120](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261120)
competition. This is version 3 of the entry. Version 2 was submitted and
scored a 0.9778 judge rate (rank #2,554 of 18,803); v3 keeps every verified
fact from v2 and revises the presentation on the reader's behalf — a
masthead that actually tells the day's news, a two-sentence caption on
every featured story, a one-sentence caption on every story in the list,
and a one-line theme for each region, so someone who missed the day can
follow it from the picture alone.

Every story here happened, or was first reported, on Friday 20 November
2026. Sources are named beside each story. Nothing is invented: where a
story was still developing at press time, the DATE CHECK box at the foot
of the page says so.

## Design

A newspaper front page on warm paper (248, 243, 231) with a navy
masthead, red rules and accents, serif headlines (DejaVu Serif Bold) and
sans-serif body text (DejaVu Sans). The layout is drawn at 1024×1024 in
Python with Pillow and saved as a PNG under 1 MiB.

What changed from v2 (rank #2,554, judge 0.9778):

- **A headline that reports.** The masthead now carries a two-line news
  headline naming the day's biggest stories instead of a bare title, so
  the page's subject is clear before a single card is read.
- **Captions with substance.** Every featured story gets a two-sentence
  caption (who, what, and what it means); every story in the "32 more"
  list gets a one-sentence caption instead of a bare headline. The grid
  was redesigned around that text: shorter picture strips on the medium
  cards, tighter leading in the list, and a taller DATE CHECK box.
- **A theme per region.** One line under the region bar sums up what the
  day was about in each of the seven regions.
- **Sharper pictures.** All 12 AI illustrations were regenerated with
  more specific prompts — the scene, the light, the mood, the key
  elements of each story — so each picture reads as its story at a
  glance. The Van Gogh card is still drawn in code, as before.
- **DATE CHECK, fuller.** It now names all three stories left out as
  not-20-November news (the Microsoft 365 outage of 12 Nov, the
  Kramatorsk bus strike of 8 Oct, the FAA ground stop of 13 Nov), the
  two 19 November stories (the US Senate energy bill vote, the APEC
  leaders' Thursday sessions), and what was still unconfirmed at press
  time (the full Kyiv casualty toll, the final COP31 text).

## How to run

The program is a one-line Node 22 script (standard library only) that
copies `entry.png` to the output directory:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

Without `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` it writes to `/output/entry.png`, as the
grader expects. Both runs write the same bytes.
